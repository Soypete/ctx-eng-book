How to review one source

  1. Open the module’s ledger entry and its linked notes file.
  2. Open the paper, standard, or report URL listed in the queue item.
  3. Check whether the source supports the exact claim—not merely the general topic.
  4. Record one decision: reviewed — cite, reviewed — do not cite, or reviewed — revise claim.
  5. Add one sentence explaining the decision, especially limitations, version, or scope.
  6. Move to the next source. When a batch is finished, tell me the module IDs and I will promote approved sources into prose and references.

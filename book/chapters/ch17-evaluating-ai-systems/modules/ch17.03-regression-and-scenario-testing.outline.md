# Regression and Scenario Testing

## Argument
Regression tests compare context-to-outcome invariants and distributions rather than prose strings.

## Scenario-to-regression flow

Mermaid flow from incident or requirement through versioned scenario, paired runs, stochastic repetition, nearby mutations, slice comparison, and stable/refreshed suites.

## Beats
1. Specify coverage, authority, semantics, state, dependencies, allowed variation, and outcome.
2. Pair baseline and candidate and repeat stochastic stages.
3. Protect the versions and trace references needed for replay.
4. Convert incidents into minimal cases and nearby mutations.
5. Maintain stable and refreshed case sets.
6. Treat curriculum notebooks as teaching assets and keep runnable harnesses in a maintained source repository.

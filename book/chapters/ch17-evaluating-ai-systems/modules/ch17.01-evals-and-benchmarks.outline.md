# Evals and Benchmarks

## Argument
Improvement is a bounded claim about a context-to-outcome pipeline: lexical coverage and authority, semantic correctness, pragmatic authorization and action validity, model quality, and outcome reliability all require evidence.

## Beats
1. Evaluate the five context-to-outcome checkpoints.
2. Build versioned cases from requirements and failures.
3. Prefer deterministic checks; validate human and model judges.
4. Compare paired repeated trials and predefined thresholds.
5. Use benchmarks only for their intended construct.

# Reliability Metrics and Failure Budgets

## Argument
Reliability requires explicit stage indicators, end-to-end objectives, and consequence-aware budgets tied to eligible populations.

## Indicator-to-decision flow

Mermaid flow from eligible interactions through indicators, objectives, budget consumption, drift review, release action, and updated cases and controls.

## Beats
1. Measure lexical, semantic, pragmatic, model, and outcome boundaries by slice.
2. Define budget as permitted shortfall from an objective.
3. Keep non-fungible high-consequence failures separate.
4. Make the denominator, window, exclusions, and slices part of the metric contract.
5. Govern releases with consumption and continuous evaluator review.
6. Treat drift as an investigation trigger and keep runnable monitoring in a maintained source repository.

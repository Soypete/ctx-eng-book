# The Future of AI Engineering

## Argument
Context engineering creates distinct responsibilities across data, retrieval, state, authorization, platform operations, and evaluation; ownership should follow risk and scale rather than a predetermined set of job titles.

## Why it matters (reliability goal)
Tie to Pragmatics pillar: unowned component boundaries become untested failure boundaries. Specialization can improve reliability when system scale and risk justify it, while small teams may combine roles.

# Engineering the Context Boundaries

## Argument
Context engineering names responsibilities across source data, retrieval, semantics, state, authorization, platform operations, and evaluation; ownership should follow failure boundaries, risk, and scale rather than predetermined job titles.

## Why it matters (reliability goal)
Use the Lexicon → Semantics → Pragmatics spine: unowned source, meaning, and policy boundaries become untested failure boundaries. Specialization can improve reliability when scale and risk justify it, while small teams may combine responsibilities.

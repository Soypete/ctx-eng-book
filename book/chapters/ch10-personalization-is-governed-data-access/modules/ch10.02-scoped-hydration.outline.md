# Scoped Hydration Across Stores

## Argument
Scoped hydration coordinates one authenticated task across heterogeneous enforcement semantics: Lexicon controls eligible sources, Semantics controls valid combinations and interpretations, and Pragmatics controls permitted use. The product promise is engineered context, not “memory” as a feature; indexes, paths, caches, or service credentials must not weaken policy.

# Personalization as Governed Retrieval

## Argument
Personalization binds authenticated identity and purpose to enforceable retrieval decisions before user-specific state becomes context.

## Reliability goal
Prevent prompts or model-generated attributes from acting as authorization.

## Sequence
1. Establish personalization as data access. 2. separate policy decision and enforcement. 3. present ABAC as one model. 4. govern personal-data purposes and derivation. 5. test identity and revocation failures.

## Example
Trusted retrieval authorization envelope.

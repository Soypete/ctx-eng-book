# Guardrails and Ontology-Based Validation

## Argument
A guardrail is an enforceable check at a named input, retrieval, output, execution, or postcondition boundary.

## Reliability goal
Prevent vague “guardrail” claims and contain invalid or unauthorized behavior before the protected boundary is crossed.

## Sequence
1. Require enforcement semantics. 2. Place checks at their boundaries. 3. separate ontology, graph, shape, schema, and policy roles. 4. define failure outcomes. 5. test escape and false rejection.

## Example
Expense-action validation chain.

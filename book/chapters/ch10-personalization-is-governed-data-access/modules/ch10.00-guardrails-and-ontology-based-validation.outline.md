# Guardrails and Ontology-Based Validation

## Argument
A guardrail is an enforceable check at a named input, retrieval, output, execution, or postcondition boundary. Chapter 10 maps those checks from Lexicon (available and authoritative data), through Semantics (validated entities and relations), to Pragmatics (permitted use and action).

## Reliability goal
Prevent vague “guardrail” claims and contain invalid or unauthorized behavior before the protected boundary is crossed.

## Sequence
1. Apply the context engineering framework. 2. Show the curriculum-aligned guardrail ladder in Mermaid. 3. Require enforcement semantics. 4. Place checks at their boundaries. 5. Separate ontology, graph, shape, schema, and policy roles. 6. Define failure outcomes and test escape and false rejection.

# Information Extraction Pipelines

## Argument

Information extraction creates derived, queryable state; extracted entities, events, and relations remain provenance-bearing proposals until identity, schema, source entailment, and domain policy validate them.

## Reliability goal

Prevent extraction from laundering user claims, ambiguous mentions, negated events, or model additions into authoritative facts.

## Sequence

1. Establish when structured extraction is useful and why output is proposed state.
2. Distinguish mention detection, entity resolution, relation extraction, event extraction, and OpenIE.
3. Preserve modality, attribution, conditions, time, and source spans beyond triples.
4. Separate source retention, extraction, resolution, validation, promotion, and indexing.
5. Evaluate errors by consequence and extractor version.
6. Retrieve derived records with their supporting evidence and lifecycle.

## Architecture example

An acquisition statement demonstrates how a simple triple destroys modality, attribution, identity uncertainty, and conditions; a richer JSON record preserves them.

## Corrections from draft

- Unstructured text is not inherently noise, and IE is not required for every document retrieval task.
- Entity detection does not resolve identity or authorize access.
- LLM output absent from a source is generation, not grounded extraction.
- A task-specific knowledge-graph QA benchmark does not establish a general IE or graph advantage.
- Product inventory removed in favor of selection and validation principles.

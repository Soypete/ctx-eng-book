# ch07.06 Information Extraction Pipelines

## Extraction Creates Derived State

Documents are already usable context. Information extraction is valuable when the system needs to filter, join, aggregate, traverse, or enforce policy over facts that are only expressed in text. It converts passages into proposed entities, events, attributes, and relationships that can be indexed and queried.

The word *proposed* matters. If a support ticket says, “The customer claims the package never arrived,” an extractor can produce a delivery claim. It has not established that the package was not delivered. The record's authority remains the customer's statement, and its provenance remains the ticket passage.

An extraction pipeline therefore creates derived state. Its output needs a schema, source links, versioning, validation, and a lifecycle just like the memory transformations in Chapter 6.

## Choose the Representation the Query Needs

Different extraction tasks answer different questions:

- **entity mention detection** finds spans that refer to people, organizations, products, locations, dates, or domain-specific concepts;
- **entity resolution** links a mention to a known identity—or deliberately leaves it unresolved;
- **relation extraction** proposes a typed relationship between resolved or unresolved entities;
- **event extraction** records an occurrence with participants, time, location, action, and outcome;
- **open information extraction** proposes relations without committing to a domain schema.

Mention detection is not entity resolution. Extracting “Jordan” does not establish which customer, employee, or country the passage means. Relation extraction is also not authorization: learning that a person is associated with an account does not grant a caller access to it.

Open extraction is useful for exploration when the domain is still being learned. Its tuples should not flow directly into authoritative context. Map recurring relations into a reviewed schema, define their semantics and constraints, and validate them against task-specific examples. Otherwise surface variations such as `works for`, `employed by`, and `joined` fragment the same concept while superficially similar phrases collapse different ones.

## Preserve More Than a Triple

Subject–predicate–object is often too small a representation for reliable use. Negation, modality, attribution, quantity, and time change meaning:

```text
“Dana said Acme may acquire Beta after regulatory approval.”
```

The triple `Acme — acquires → Beta` converts a reported possibility into a completed fact. A safer extraction record carries the distinctions the downstream decision needs:

```json
{
  "type": "acquisition_proposal",
  "subject": {"text": "Acme", "entity_id": null},
  "object": {"text": "Beta", "entity_id": null},
  "modality": "possible",
  "status": "conditional",
  "condition": "regulatory approval",
  "attributed_to": "Dana",
  "source_document": "doc-481",
  "source_span": [120, 178],
  "extractor_version": "events-v3",
  "validation_status": "proposed"
}
```

This record is longer because the source claim is more complex than a triple. Schema precision should follow the decisions the data will support, not a desire to make every sentence graph-shaped.

## Separate Extraction from Promotion

A reliable ingestion path has distinct stages:

1. retain the source document and immutable locator;
2. normalize format without erasing offsets or document structure;
3. propose mentions, entities, relations, and events;
4. resolve identities within tenant and domain scope;
5. validate schema, constraints, temporal fields, and source entailment;
6. route uncertain or high-consequence proposals for review;
7. promote accepted records into a queryable representation;
8. index them and record the index version;
9. monitor corrections and rebuild derived state when the extractor changes.

Rules, statistical models, and language models can all participate. Use deterministic parsers for stable formats, trained models for established labels, and constrained language-model extraction where linguistic variation makes rules brittle. The choice is empirical. Evaluate it on representative documents, including negation, ambiguous identities, unusual formatting, and adversarial content.

A model's parameters are not a source for a document extraction. If the output contains a fact absent from the input, the extractor has generated rather than extracted it. Validate source entailment and require exact passage locators so unsupported additions can be detected.

## Measure Errors at the Decision Boundary

Entity and relation precision and recall are useful, but aggregate scores can hide costly errors. A false product mention may add irrelevant context. A false account identity may leak data. A missed negation may reverse an action. Break evaluation down by entity type, relation, source format, language, ambiguity, and consequence.

Useful operational measures include:

- unresolved and incorrectly merged entity rates;
- field-level precision and recall on reviewed samples;
- temporal normalization error;
- negation, modality, and attribution preservation;
- schema-rejection and human-review rates;
- provenance and source-span coverage;
- correction propagation and re-index latency;
- downstream retrieval and task outcomes by extractor version.

Use a shadow deployment before replacing an extractor. Run old and new versions over the same sources, compare proposed records, review high-impact differences, and rebuild indexes from retained source documents when promoting the new version.

## Close the Loop to Context Assembly

At query time, the context pipeline should know that an extracted record is derived and should preserve the identity scope and semantic role assigned during validation. It can retrieve the structured event for filtering and then include the supporting passage when evidence matters. If the record is corrected or its source is deleted, the derived representation and indexes must follow the lifecycle policy.

Extraction improves reliability when it makes implicit source structure queryable without laundering claims into facts. It harms reliability when generated relations lose attribution, identity, time, or uncertainty. The system boundary is straightforward: extractors propose structured state; validation and domain policy decide what may become durable knowledge.

# Small Composable Systems

## Argument
Composition improves reliability only when components align with enforceable boundaries and their contracts make distributed complexity observable.

## Reliability goal
Gain isolated testing and failure containment without creating gratuitous services, version skew, and retry cascades.

## Evidence
- [Ritchie & Thompson, 1974](https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/about/dennis-m-ritchie/cacm.html) documents the UNIX system.
- The familiar programming maxims are attributed to McIlroy, Pinson, and Tague's 1978 UNIX foreword, not the 1974 paper.

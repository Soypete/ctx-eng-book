# Ontology-Guided Information Extraction

## Argument

An ontology should compile into a task-specific extraction contract that narrows proposals while preserving ambiguity, provenance, and deliberate schema evolution.

## Reliability goal

Reduce invalid extracted structure without laundering prompt compliance into validated knowledge or discarding novel evidence silently.

## Sequence

1. Constrain rather than eliminate the proposal space.
2. Compile a versioned task subset of the ontology and show the contract boundary in Mermaid.
3. Connect the curriculum's spaCy, ontology, and model comparison to candidate generation and schema comparison.
4. Separate guidance, deterministic validation, resolution, and source entailment.
5. Route novelty through governed model evolution.
6. Compare against a baseline and measure consequential errors.

## Corrections

Ontology guidance is not universal IE, domain/range checks do not prove truth, and LLM training knowledge is not source-grounded extraction.

# Guardrails for Extraction Validation

## Argument

Validation establishes conformance to evidence, identity, authority, and application policy—not universal truth—and promotion must reflect consequence as well as uncertainty.

## Reliability goal

Prevent plausible structured proposals from becoming retrievable knowledge without source support and a recorded acceptance decision.

## Sequence

1. Narrow what validation proves.
2. Show structural, semantic, evidence, identity, authority, and promotion gates in Mermaid.
3. Connect validation evaluation to the curriculum's RAGAS testset/datasheet and DeepEval release gate.
4. Validate structure, semantics, source entailment, identity, authority, conflicts, and promotion.
5. Keep uncertainty signals separate and calibrate decisions empirically.
6. Preserve the complete decision record.
7. Measure false acceptance and downstream escape.

## Corrections

Ontology checks are not truth checks; graph absence is not contradiction under open-world semantics; model confidence does not justify automatic acceptance.

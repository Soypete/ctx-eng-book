# Entity Resolution and Relationship Traversal

## Argument

Graph retrieval is reliable only when entity equivalence is a reversible, evidence-bearing assertion and traversal is a scoped, authorized query plan.

## Reliability goal

Prevent false identity merges and uncontrolled path expansion from leaking or corrupting model context.

## Sequence

1. Show that precise edges fail when endpoints are wrong.
2. Preserve source records and version canonical-identity assertions.
3. Tier exact, fuzzy, and graph signals by evidentiary strength.
4. Define traversal scope, authorization, time, budget, and provenance.
5. Separate known path patterns from bounded discovery.
6. Evaluate resolution and traversal end to end with failure injection.

## Example

A resolution assertion records source identity, method, evidence, effective time, policy version, and reversible status.

## Corrections from draft

- Matching email, phone, name, or neighbor does not prove identity.
- Canonicalization does not make one node authoritative for every attribute.
- Authorization applies during traversal, not only to final results.
- Graph traversal and vector search are not a complete structured/exploratory dichotomy.
- Product survey and unverified benchmark attribution removed.

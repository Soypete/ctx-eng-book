# One-Shot Execution, Loops, and Delegated Workflows

## Argument
One-shot, iterative, and delegated workflows must be compared by full expected outcome cost, including their context and state work.

## Beats
1. Extra attempts require observable progress.
2. Delegation needs partition, validation, merge, authority, and tree budgets.
3. Measure deployed memory and capacity under context length and concurrency.

## Reliability takeaway
Additional calls earn their cost only through measured failure reduction.

# Cost-Aware Extraction Pipeline Design

## Argument
Extraction economics span ingestion, extraction, resolution, validation, storage, serving, correction, and deletion.

## Beats
1. Maintain a versioned stage ledger and trace artifact lineage from source to served evidence.
2. Reconcile corrections and deletions through explicit reprocessing.
3. Cache only under explicit source, extractor, schema, and policy versions.
4. Optimize cost per accepted assertion and successful downstream task.
5. Retain release evidence and evolve routes through ablations and review evidence.

## Reliability takeaway
The cheapest reliable pipeline is a replaceable policy proven against explicit outcome constraints.

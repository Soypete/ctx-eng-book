# Extraction Method Selection: Rules, Models, and LLMs

## Argument
Route assertion types to the least expensive method that meets their consequence-aware contract.

## Beats
1. Specify grounding, schema, provenance, abstention, and consequence.
2. Establish rules, parsers, specialized models, LLMs, and review as candidate routes.
3. Apply shared validation and versioned promotion.
4. Evaluate both each route and the router.

## Reliability takeaway
Method selection is an empirical systems decision, not a capability contest.

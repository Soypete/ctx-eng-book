# Input, Transformation, and Effect Lineage

## Argument
Lineage connects authoritative sources, semantic transformations, policy decisions, model inputs, effects, and outputs without requiring unsafe duplication of content.

## Beats
1. Record source authority, semantic transformations, prompt rendering, and admitted-context manifests.
2. Separate retrieval candidates, authorization, ranking, and admission outcomes.
3. Follow model proposals and pragmatic policy checks through validation, authorization, dispatch, receipts, and state.
4. Test reconstruction through incident drills and isolated replay.

## Reliability takeaway
Lineage earns its cost when an investigator can reproduce the relevant boundary failure.

# Observability, Evaluation, and Cost Control

## Argument
Production readiness is the ability to detect, diagnose, contain, evaluate, and reverse failures under explicit cost and outcome constraints.

## Observe-to-decision feedback loop

Mermaid flow from run-manifest observation through diagnosis, protected reproduction, one-boundary change, evaluation, guarded rollout, budget measurement, and retention or rollback.

## Beats
1. Preserve a governed run manifest.
2. Evaluate components, scenarios, behavior, and regressions.
3. Couple reliability objectives with lifecycle cost.
4. Require ownership, SLOs, migrations, and rollback.
5. Keep runnable operational assets in a maintained source repository.

# Semantic and Retrieval Infrastructure

## Argument
Retrieval converts task, identity, purpose, and budget into an evidence manifest with explicit uncertainty.

## Retrieval-policy flow

Mermaid flow from task and policy inputs through multiple routes, typed candidates, authority checks, fusion, manifest admission, and evaluation.

## Beats
1. Plan required evidence and eligible routes.
2. Reduce the candidate space with authorization before ranking.
3. Preserve route scores and provenance without false score equivalence.
4. Distinguish pairwise ranking, Bradley–Terry aggregation, tree search, and Thompson sampling.
5. Make fusion, deduplication, budget, and admission reasons explicit.
6. Separate found, unauthorized, stale, conflict, and failure outcomes.
7. Evaluate candidates, admission, support, cost, and downstream outcome.

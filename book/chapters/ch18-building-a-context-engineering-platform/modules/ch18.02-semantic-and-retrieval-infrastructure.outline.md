# Semantic and Retrieval Infrastructure

## Argument
Retrieval converts task, identity, purpose, and budget into an evidence manifest with explicit uncertainty.

## Beats
1. Plan required evidence and eligible routes.
2. Preserve route scores and provenance without false score equivalence.
3. Separate found, unauthorized, stale, conflict, and failure outcomes.
4. Evaluate candidates, admission, support, cost, and downstream outcome.

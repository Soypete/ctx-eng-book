# Context Engineering as the Research Phase

## Argument
Context engineering makes a task observable and supplies the contracts, data, evaluations, and controls that remain necessary after model modification.

## Beats
1. Use a reversible, evidence-producing decision process.
2. Order experiments by information gain, consequence, and lifecycle cost.
3. Preserve retrieval, authorization, tools, state, provenance, and evaluation.
4. Keep changing, governed facts outside weights.

## Reliability takeaway
Model modification is one implementation choice inside a reliable system, not a replacement for it.

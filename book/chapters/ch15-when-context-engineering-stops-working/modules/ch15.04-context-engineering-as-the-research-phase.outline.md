# Context Engineering as the Research Phase

## Argument
Context engineering makes a task observable across Lexicon, Semantics, and Pragmatics, and supplies the contracts, data, evaluations, and controls that remain necessary after any model modification.

## Beats
1. Test Lexicon, Semantics, and Pragmatics plus retrieval, authorization, orchestration, and evaluation before assigning a failure to the model.
2. Produce an evidence packet through a reversible, evidence-producing decision loop.
3. Order interventions by information gain, consequence, and lifecycle cost.
4. Preserve retrieval, authorization, tools, state, provenance, and evaluation after model selection or modification.
5. Keep changing, governed facts outside weights.

## Reliability takeaway
Context engineering spans NLP systems beyond agents; model modification is one implementation choice inside a reliable system, not a replacement for it.

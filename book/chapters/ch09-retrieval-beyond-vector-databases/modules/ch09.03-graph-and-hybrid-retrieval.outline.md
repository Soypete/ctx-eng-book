# Graph and Hybrid Retrieval

## Argument
Graph retrieval generates governed path candidates when relationships are
evidence; hybrid retrieval selects and fuses only the candidate generators
required by the task. Both remain dependent on authority, semantics, scope,
freshness, and evaluation.

## Reliability goal
Prevent relationship inference from similarity and prevent “hybrid” from becoming unbounded fan-out.

## Sequence
1. Retrieve explicit paths and show the graph/hybrid route in Mermaid. 2. Connect the curriculum's steelman baseline and graph comparison. 3. Separate temporal order, causality, and authorization. 4. Plan optional candidate generators. 5. Fuse ranks and deduplicate with lineage. 6. Evaluate with hit rate, MRR, and ablation. 7. Attribute graph-route misses (missing edge, unresolved entity, depth/type limit, authorization) and treat reported graph lifts as hypotheses for the ablation.

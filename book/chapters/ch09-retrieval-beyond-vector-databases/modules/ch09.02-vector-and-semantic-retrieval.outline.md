# Vector and Semantic Retrieval

## Argument
Dense retrieval is a versioned candidate generator whose reliability depends
on authority, semantics, scope, freshness, and evaluation, implemented through
evidence-aligned chunks, enforceable metadata, canonical sources, lifecycle,
and evaluated index tradeoffs.

## Reliability goal
Recover paraphrased evidence without confusing similarity with authority or allowing derived indexes to escape governance.

## Sequence
1. Define candidate generation and show the dense path in Mermaid. 2. Connect the curriculum's embed→nearest chunks→prompt sequence and its `k` comparison. 3. Chunk around evidence. 4. Filter by scoped metadata. 5. Link to canonical content. 6. Tune and evaluate. 7. Design from queries backward.

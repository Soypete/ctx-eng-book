# Authorization as the Pragmatics Boundary

## Argument
Authorization is the Pragmatics boundary: constrain who may use which
information or perform which action, for what purpose and time. Apply it to
every language-system operation.

## Reliability goal
Reduce disclosure and side-effect blast radius without treating agents as the
category boundary or claiming permissions cause every failure.

## Sequence
1. Limit authority. 2. separate four scopes. 3. use multiple enforcement mechanisms. 4. distinguish retrieval and action. 5. test grant misuse.

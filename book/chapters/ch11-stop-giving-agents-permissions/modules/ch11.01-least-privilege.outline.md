# Least Privilege for Model-Driven Systems

## Argument
Constrain principal, resource, action, time, and quantity for every model-proposed operation to contain inevitable failures.

## Reliability goal
Reduce disclosure and side-effect blast radius without claiming permissions cause every failure.

## Sequence
1. Limit authority. 2. separate four scopes. 3. use multiple enforcement mechanisms. 4. distinguish retrieval and action. 5. test grant misuse.

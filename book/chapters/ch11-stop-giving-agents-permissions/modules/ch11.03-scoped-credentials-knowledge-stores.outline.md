# Scoped Credentials and Trusted Brokers

## Argument
Trusted brokers keep credentials outside language-visible context and issue
narrow, purpose-bound authority to trusted executors.

## Reliability goal
Prevent secret disclosure, replay, confused delegation, and stale authority.

## Sequence
1. keep secrets out of context. 2. broker exact authority. 3. compare reference/self-contained validation. 4. attenuate delegation. 5. operate and failure-test the broker.

## Readiness
Edited; “knowledge store,” self-enforcing token, fixed lifetime, and default attestation claims removed.

# Context Engineering as the Research Phase Notes

## ch15.04 — Sources queued for review

- Raoni Lourenço, Juliana Freire, and Dennis Shasha (2020). *Debugging Machine Learning Pipelines*. arXiv (version 1). https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04640
  - **Why it matters here:** treats pipeline failures as provenance and root-cause problems, supporting evidence packets that preserve what changed and where a failure originated.
- Inioluwa Deborah Raji et al. (2020). *Closing the AI Accountability Gap: Defining an End-to-End Framework for Internal Algorithmic Auditing*. Proceedings of FAT*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.00973
  - **Why it matters here:** proposes stage-by-stage audit documents across the development lifecycle, supporting the claim that evaluation and accountability continue through deployment.
- Nithya Sambasivan et al. (2021). *“Everyone wants to do the model work, not the data work”: Data Cascades in High-Stakes AI*. Proceedings of CHI. https://doi.org/10.1145/3411764.3445518
  - **Why it matters here:** reports compounding downstream effects from neglected data practices, supporting context engineering as ongoing research into data and system boundaries.
- Eric Breck et al. (2017). *The ML Test Score: A Rubric for ML Production Readiness and Technical Debt Reduction*. IEEE. https://research.google.com/pubs/archive/aad9f93b86b7addfea4c419b9100c6cdd26cacea.pdf
  - **Why it matters here:** provides production-readiness tests and monitoring practices that remain necessary after model selection or modification.

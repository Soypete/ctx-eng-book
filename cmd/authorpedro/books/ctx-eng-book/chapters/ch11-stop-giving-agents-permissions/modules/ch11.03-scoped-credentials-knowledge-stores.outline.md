# Scoped Credentials and Knowledge Stores

## Argument
Credentials must be stored, retrieved, and managed separately from context assembly—never in prompts. Knowledge stores provide issuance, storage, retrieval, validation, and revocation.

## Why it matters (reliability goal)
Credentials in prompts are visible to models, logged, and impossible to revoke. Separation ensures credentials never flow through context pipeline.

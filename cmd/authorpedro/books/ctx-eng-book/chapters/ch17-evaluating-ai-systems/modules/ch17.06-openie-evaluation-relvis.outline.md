# OpenIE Evaluation (RelVis)

## Argument
RelVis provides standardized evaluation for Open Information Extraction—benchmark datasets and metrics when there's no schema to compare against.

## Why it matters (reliability goal)
Tie to Semantics pillar: OpenIE extracts relations without predefined schemas, making evaluation challenging. RelVis provides standardized comparison.

## Beats

1. **The RelVis Benchmark**
   - 4 datasets: 3 news + 1 Wikipedia
   - 4,522 labeled sentences, 11,243 OIE relations
   - Evaluates: ClausIE, OpenIE 4.2, Stanford OpenIE, PredPatt

2. **Error Analysis**
   - Common error classes: missing arguments, wrong arguments, wrong relation, granularity, noise
   - Analysis of 749 n-ary tuples

3. **Limitations**
   - OpenIE without ontology = lots of tuples, little usable structure
   - Domain: news/Wikipedia focused
   - Not actively maintained

## Conclusion to reach
The reader should believe that OpenIE evaluation is possible without predefined schemas—but needs standardized benchmarks like RelVis to compare systems.

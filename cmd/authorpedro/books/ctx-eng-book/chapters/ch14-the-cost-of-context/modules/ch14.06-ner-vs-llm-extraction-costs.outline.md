# NER vs LLM Extraction Costs

## Argument
The choice between traditional NER and LLM extraction is a classic cost tradeoff: traditional methods have high upfront cost but low ongoing costs, while LLMs have low upfront cost but accumulate per-inference costs at scale.

## Why it matters (reliability goal)
Tie to Pragmatics pillar: extraction method choice directly impacts cost structure. Understanding the tradeoff enables matching the method to the task's reliability requirements.

## Beats

1. **Traditional NER: High Capex, Low Opex** — evidence: relation-extraction.md
   - Requires labeled data, model training, pipeline development
   - Inference is fast, cheap, deterministic once trained
   - Domain-specific strength via fine-tuning

2. **LLM Extraction: Low Capex, High Opex** — evidence: relation-extraction.md
   - In-context learning, no training required
   - Per-inference API costs accumulate at scale
   - Flexibility: extracts novel entity types without retraining

3. **The Break-Even Point** — evidence: ie-ner.md
   - High volume → traditional NER amortizes training cost
   - Static domains → trained models; volatile domains → LLM flexibility
   - Accuracy requirements: fine-tuned vs "good enough"

## Conclusion to reach
The reader should believe that extraction method selection is a cost decision first. Match the method to volume, domain stability, and reliability requirements.

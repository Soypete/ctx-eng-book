# Extraction Method Selection: Rule-Based vs LLM

## Argument
Not every extraction task needs an LLM. Cost-aware method selection means matching the approach to task complexity, volume, and reliability requirements—using rule-based methods for deterministic tasks and LLMs for ambiguous ones.

## Why it matters (reliability goal)
Tie to Pragmatics pillar: the pragmatic choice isn't always the most powerful tool, it's the one that meets reliability requirements at minimum cost.

## Beats

1. **Rule-Based Extraction** — evidence: ie-ner.md, relation-extraction.md
   - Fixed-format data, high-volume stable schemas
   - Cost profile: high upfront, low runtime, nearly free at scale

2. **LLM Extraction** — evidence: ie-ner.md
   - Unstructured/Variable text, novel domain exploration
   - Cost profile: low upfront, per-token runtime, linear scaling

3. **Hybrid Approach: The Pragmatic Middle** — evidence: ie-ner.md
   - Rule-based pre-processing → LLM for ambiguous → validation layer
   - Don't use a sledgehammer to crack a nut

4. **The Efficiency Principle** — evidence: ie-ner.md
   - "You don't need perfect extraction—you need good enough extraction with good guardrails"
   - Match method to cost-reliability tradeoff

## Conclusion to reach
The reader should believe that method selection is a engineering decision, not a capability contest. Choose the cheapest method that meets reliability requirements.

# Ontology-Guided Information Extraction

## Argument
Ontologies are foundational for information extraction—they constrain what can be extracted to valid schema elements, preventing noisy or irrelevant output. This is the "shift left" motion: define semantics at design-time, not during inference.

## Why it matters (reliability goal)
Tie to Pragmatics pillar: ontology guides extraction AND validates output. The graph becomes a guardrail, rejecting invalid extractions before they reach the model.

## Beats

1. **The Extraction Problem** — evidence: ledger (Ontologies foundational for RE - relation-extraction)
   - Without constraints: IE produces noise, inconsistency
   - Ontology provides entity types, relationship types, cardinality

2. **Shift Left Motion** — evidence: ledger (Relation extraction - shift left)
   - Move semantics to design-time
   - Define ontology in config
   - Keep data contracts clean

3. **LLM-Based Extraction** — evidence: ledger (LLMs enabling novel entity extraction - knowledge graph book)
   - IE with LLMs = knowledge distillation
   - LLMs can identify novel entity types, classify into ontology using ICL
   - Provide ontology as context, extract only what's defined

4. **Validation Guardrails** — evidence: existing prose draft
   - Query graph to ensure consistency with known facts
   - Reject violations of domain/range constraints
   - Track provenance of extracted facts

## Conclusion to reach
The reader should believe that ontology-guided extraction transforms IE from "extract everything and filter" to "extract only what's valid"—reducing noise and enabling reliable knowledge graph population.
